### Full Description (Max 4000 chars)
Transform your Android tablet into a professional graphics drawing pad, screen annotator, and precision wireless trackpad for your Mac. 

TabSync Studio gives digital artists, educators, developers, presenters, and business professionals the power to annotate, write, and draw across any Mac application or browser with zero lag and hardware pressure sensitivity.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

🎨 PROFESSIONAL DRAWING PAD & SCREEN ANNOTATOR
• Draw directly over any Mac application, browser, presentation, or PDF.
• S-Pen & Stylus Support: True hardware pressure dynamics modulate stroke width and ink opacity.
• Smart Palm Rejection: Rest your hand comfortably on your tablet screen without accidental marks.
• Vector Inking Tools: Fine Pen, Semi-transparent Marker/Highlighter, Presenter Laser with fading comet trail, and Eraser.
• High Refresh Rate Rendering: Silky smooth 60–120Hz canvas response.
• Stepped Stroke Widths & Studio Color Palettes with instant bidirectional sync.

🖱️ PRECISION WIRELESS TRACKPAD & DIGITIZER
• Smooth relative cursor control with zero jumping.
• Tap to left click, two-finger tap to right click, and two-finger drag to scroll.
• Dedicated Drag Lock toggle: Select code, drag windows, and highlight text effortlessly without lifting fingers.

🔒 ENTERPRISE-GRADE LOCAL SECURITY
• 100% Local Network Operation: Zero cloud relays, zero external tracking, zero third-party servers.
• Cryptographic Device Pairing: Secure pairing via dynamic QR code or 6-digit PIN.
• Device Authorization Management: Review and revoke connected tablets directly from your Mac menu bar.
• Stuck Input Protection: Disconnecting automatically releases all held mouse buttons and modifier keys.

📸 ONE-CLICK PRESENTATION TOOLS
• Quick Screenshot / Annotation capture directly to your Mac clipboard and Desktop.
• Undo / Redo history with multi-device synchronization.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Launch TabSync Studio on your Mac (download from https://tabsync.studio).
2. Open TabSync Studio on your Android tablet.
3. Scan the Mac QR code or enter the 6-digit PIN.
4. Start drawing, annotating, or navigating immediately!

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
• Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher (optimized for Android 13 & 14 tablets).
• Mac with macOS 11.0 Big Sur or later (Apple Silicon & Intel).
• Both devices connected to the same local Wi-Fi network.

PRIVACY GUARANTEE:
TabSync Studio operates exclusively on your local network. No strokes, cursor movements, personal data, or keystrokes are ever sent to external cloud servers.
